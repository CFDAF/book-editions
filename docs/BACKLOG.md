# BACKLOG — what is still open

The build is finished (2026-09-28). This is every item still open. **Append**
what you notice and do not fix; strike a row through with what settled it when
it is done. Book and case ids (N06, H05, P02 …) are those of
`docs/DECISIONS.md`.

Three kinds of row, and they are not the same kind of work:

- **Deferred** — work that could not be done where it was noticed. Each names
  what it needs.
- **Open — the user's call** — a decision, not a task. Nothing here may be
  started until the user has taken it.
- **Operational** — something observed that no change owns. Nothing to do until
  the situation recurs.
